Space Traveller

Design a spacecraft, launch it anywhere from the Sun to Pluto, and bring the science home.

    Costs, masses and scores are simplified for play. Distances, sunlight levels, trip times and the "real mission" facts come from NASA missions. Planet images come from the NASA Photojournal, credited on each card. Sources include NASA Juno and the JPL Perseverance press kit. Made for NASA Space Apps 2026.
